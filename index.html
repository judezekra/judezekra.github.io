About

I like the messy middle between people and systems.

I'm studying Management Information Systems at UGA with certificates in Artificial Intelligence and Legal Studies, carrying a 3.87 GPA as a Presidential Scholar and Zell Miller Scholar.

Most of what I enjoy sits where business questions meet technical ones: building an AI chatbot so students can actually find campus resources, designing a prompt pipeline to shortlist partner companies for my department, or working out whether a healthcare model can scale before anyone writes code. This summer I'm at Intuit as a Business Development Intern.

The legal side isn't a detour from the technical one. As more decisions get handed to models, someone has to think clearly about privacy, data ethics, and accountability — and it helps if that person also understands how the system was built. Outside of coursework I'm VP of Finance for Wishmakers on Campus and I referee soccer.
